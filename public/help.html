Tickweek ✕

How it works

Tick off your daily targets. Weigh in every Monday. Watch the weight come down.

1 · Tick your targets

  • Tap a box when you've done that target for the day. Tap again to undo.
  • Number targets (like running or weights): tap the box and type the miles or minutes.
  • Forgot a day? Fill it in any time this week. Last week stays open until you weigh in.
  • 3/7 on a target means 3 of the 7 days this week it asks for.
  • A combined target, like Exercise from Running and Weight training, ticks itself on any day one of them is done.
  • Hit a target's goal for the week and its ticks turn into ★.

2 · Weigh in on Monday

  • Why Monday? Weekends are where plans slip. Weighing on Monday means the scales judge your weekend straight away, while you still remember what you ate.
  • Why only weekly? Your weight bounces day to day with water, salt and food. Once a week shows the real trend without the noise.
  • Tip: same scales, first thing in the morning, before breakfast. Then every Monday is comparable.
  • Missed it? Enter it later in the week. It still counts, marked as late.

3 · Scores and rewards

  • The week in a word: Fresh week, Started, Catch-up time, On track or Great, judged on pace against your goals so far. Past weeks get Great, Good or Tough week. History shows the score as a %.
  • ★ Star day: every target with a goal done that day. The day gets a ★ at the top of the week.
  • 🏆 Perfect week: every target hit its goal for the week.
  • Streaks: days in a row for daily targets, weeks in a row for the others.
  • Weight-loss streak: Mondays in a row lower than the one before.

4 · History

  • Your weight, how much you've lost and how far to your goal.
  • Each month's weeks at a glance: one dot per target per day.
  • A month-by-month summary for the year, and your achievements.

5 · Settings

  • Add, rename, reorder or retire targets, and set how many days a week each one asks for.
  • A combined target counts other targets together. Those count towards it and have no goal of their own.
  • Set your goal weight.
  • Changes apply from this week on. Past weeks keep the targets they had.
